Privacy Policy

Elevon mobile app · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains how Lewe Tech Oy ("we", "us") handles information in the Elevon mobile app for iOS and Android.

1. Data stored on your device

Workouts, custom programs, progress, streaks, nutrition logs, calorie goals and sleep logs are stored only on your own device. Elevon has no user accounts, and we do not receive or store this data on our servers. The Elevon Coach motivator runs entirely on your device.

2. Analytics and crash reports

To keep the app stable and improve it, Elevon uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage data, such as which screens are opened, app version and general device information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports with technical details about the device and the app's state when an error occurred.

This data is not used to identify you personally, is never sold, and is not used for advertising. Google processes it as our service provider. See Firebase privacy and security.

3. Health and fitness information

Nutrition and sleep entries are health-related information. They are kept only on your device and are not shared with us or any third party.

4. Advertising

The free version of Elevon shows ads served by Google AdMob: a short video before a workout and one full-screen ad halfway through a workout. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and share your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and technical data about your device and how ads perform. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings. Subscribing to Premium turns off all ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Elevon Premium and purchases

Elevon Premium (weekly, monthly or yearly subscription) removes all ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment card details. The app only checks with the store whether your subscription is active. You can cancel at any time in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

6. XP, levels and Skip Passes

Your XP, level, streak and Skip Passes are calculated and stored only on your device.

7. Children

Elevon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or delete data related to you. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
